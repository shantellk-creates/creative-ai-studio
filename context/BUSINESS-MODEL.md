# BUSINESS-MODEL.md — How the Studio Makes Money

This file describes the business model and monetisation logic of the AI
Creative Studio. It complements `context/MASTER-CONTEXT.md` (what the business
is) with more detail on how value is meant to convert into cash.

## The core loop, restated for monetisation

```
AI Creator → Content → Distribution → Attention → Monetisation → Data → Improvement → Repeat → Scale
```

Monetisation is not a downstream side-effect of attention — it is the explicit
objective the whole loop is built to produce. Attention that does not convert
to collected cash is not treated as success.

## What counts as revenue

**Only actual cash collected counts.**

Explicitly excluded from any revenue claim or status update:
- views
- followers
- likes
- enquiries
- proposals sent
- invoices sent (unpaid)
- pipeline / forecasted deals

An invoice is not revenue until it is paid and the money is in hand. Pipeline
and forecasts may be useful for planning in Trello/ChatGPT conversations, but
must never be reported in this repository as if they were revenue.

## Current commercial focus

As of the foundation build (13 September 2026), the Studio has:
- no defined AI creator yet (Creator-001 is a framework only),
- no content produced,
- no revenue.

The immediate commercial objective is narrow and time-boxed: **£1,000+ actual
cash collected by 30 September 2026.** Business-model expansion (multiple
creators, licensing, SaaS tooling, elaborate monetisation infrastructure) is
explicitly out of scope until this is proven achievable with one creator.
